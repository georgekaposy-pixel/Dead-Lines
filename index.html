Import from Bath

  1. Open Moodle, go to Calendar, then Import or export calendars and Export calendar.
  2. Choose All events and Recent and next 60 days, then click Get calendar URL.
  3. Copy that link, paste it here and click Link Moodle. New deadlines then come in every time you open Dead-Lines.

Or click Export instead and drop the downloaded file below. Open Moodle export

Auto-tick what you've submitted (optional)
  1. In Moodle, open your profile menu, then Preferences, then Security keys.
  2. Copy the key next to Moodle mobile web service. If there isn't one, sign in to the Moodle app on your phone once, then check again.
  3. Paste it here and click Turn on.

This key lets Dead-Lines read your Moodle the way the Moodle app does. It stays in this browser on this device only, and is only ever sent to Bath's Moodle. Never share it.
